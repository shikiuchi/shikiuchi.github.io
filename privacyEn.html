Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how we handle your privacy and personal information in the apps provided by this developer (hereinafter referred to as "the App").

1. Operator

The App is developed and operated by an individual or team. For inquiries, please contact us through each app's support or the developer page on the App Store.

2. Information We Collect

The App does not send or collect any personally identifiable information to or on our servers.

  • Data used by the app's features (camera, photos, location, and other device capabilities) is generally processed only on your device and is not sent to us.
  • In-app settings and content you enter are stored only on your device (this may vary by app).

3. Purpose of Use

Since we do not collect personal information from users, there is no purpose for which we use personally collected information. Data used by the app is not sent to our servers and is either processed on your device or, except as described in "Advertising" and "Third-Party Disclosure" below, does not leave our control.

4. Advertising

Some of our apps may display advertisements. When serving ads, the ad providers (ad networks, etc.) may collect and use information such as device identifiers and usage data. How they handle this information is governed by each ad provider's privacy policy, not by us. Please refer to the relevant ad provider's or network's website for their policies.

5. Third-Party Disclosure

We do not provide your personal information to third parties except as expressly stated in this policy. The App is distributed through the App Store, so information related to downloads, purchases, and similar is handled in accordance with Apple's privacy policy.

6. Data Retention

We do not store user data on our servers, so there is no retention period on our side. Data on your device remains on the device until you delete the app or reset the device.

7. Children's Use

The App may be used by people of any age, but if a child uses it, we ask that they do so with parental consent. Depending on the app's features, we may recommend use under parental supervision.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be revised as needed. If we make material changes, we will notify you in the app or on this page. Your continued use after any update will be deemed acceptance of the changes.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or how we handle personal information, please contact us via the "Support" link on the app's page on the App Store or through the developer page.

Effective: February 2025
